About this guide

This tool is an educational aid for spotting early warning signs in common farm and companion animals. It works by comparing the symptoms you select against known symptom sets for each disease — a simple overlap check, not a diagnostic model.

  • Not a replacement for veterinary care.
  • Covers common farm and companion animal diseases.
  • Extend it yourself: the DATA object in the script holds every species, symptom and disease entry, with fields for name, symptoms, description, treatment, prevention and first aid.
